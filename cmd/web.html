Backups

{{if .BackupBucket}}

Every database is backed up daily to the R2 bucket {{.BackupBucket}} in your Cloudflare account. Its files can't be deleted or overwritten for 7 days, not even by hakobu, so a broken or compromised server can't wipe recent backups.

{{else}}

Creates a private R2 bucket in your Cloudflare account and backs up every database to it daily. Backups live off this server, so they survive losing it. R2's free plan includes 10 GB.

{{if not .CloudflareConnected}}

Needs Cloudflare connected first: cd /opt/hakobu && sudo ./hakobu setup.

{{end}} {{end}}
